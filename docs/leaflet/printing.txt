For one folded A4 sheet, choose the file ending in -A4.pdf.
Print both pages on one A4 sheet in landscape, double-sided, actual size (100%).
Choose flip on the short edge. Do not choose booklet or multiple-pages mode:
the PDF already places the pages side by side. Fold down the middle.
For manual duplex, test one sheet first because paper-feed directions vary.

Arabic and Urdu editions open from the right; the other editions open from
the left. The A4 PDFs already account for this.

The file without -A4 is the four-page reading version in normal page order.
It is sized at 148.5 x 210 mm, exactly half an A4 sheet.
