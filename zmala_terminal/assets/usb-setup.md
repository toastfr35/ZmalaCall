# First-run USB connection screen

`usb-setup.png` is the approved English installation screen. Camera → upper
blue USB 3 socket; Presenter charger → adjacent upper black USB socket, on
the rear of the Argon ONE V2 case. Installation text intentionally stays English.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then corrected using
[Nuroum's C20 photograph](https://nuroum.com/product/all-in-one-webcam-c20)
and [Argon's rear-panel reference](https://argon40.com/products/argon-one-v2-case-for-raspberry-pi-4).

“Charger”, and “Camera”; replace the camera with the reference C20 (small lens,
white tapered body, illuminated top rim and grey fabric lower speaker band).
Preserve the case, remote, arrows, highlighted sockets and navy background.

Approved typography revision: reduce the heading to approximately half its
original size and the two device labels to 65%, centered in their original
positions. Preserve all hardware and cable illustrations and fill the vacated
text area with the existing navy background.

Shown only during installed Pi enrollment when the C20 USB device is absent.
The charger is power-only and cannot be detected; detecting the C20 advances
to Bluetooth pairing or the current QR step automatically. Already-enrolled
terminals and calls are not interrupted by this installation screen.
