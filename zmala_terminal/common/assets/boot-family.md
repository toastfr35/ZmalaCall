# Boot screen

`boot-family.png` is the approved Plymouth boot composition: a bright central
Zmala logo over a subdued multilingual word cloud translating “family”. Similar
spellings are scattered across the background. The logo is part of the image.

Created and refined with the built-in image-generation tool. Final approved
revision reduces the word cloud brightness and saturation by 55% and enlarges
the central logo by 20%, layered over the words.

Plymouth scales the complete composition to fit the display while preserving its
aspect ratio, with a navy background filling any remaining space. The installer
copies it to the theme and rebuilds the initramfs when the image changes.
